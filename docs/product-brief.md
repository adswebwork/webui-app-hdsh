# Product Brief: Healthy Dog Smart House

## Problem

An owner with an outdoor or detached dog shelter may not know when conditions, water availability, or device failure need attention.

## Target user

An attentive dog owner who wants remote awareness of shelter conditions. HDSH assists the owner; it does not replace regular care or veterinary guidance.

## Smallest valuable release

A simulated dashboard that shows temperature, humidity, water-level status, device connectivity, last update, and alert history.

## Success signal

A user can correctly identify current conditions, active alerts, and a disconnected/stale device in a controlled prototype test.

## Non-goals

- Hardware integration or live telemetry
- Unattended mains heating or direct portable-heater control
- Autonomous food or water dispensing
- Live camera streaming
- Consumer-sale or safety-certification claims
